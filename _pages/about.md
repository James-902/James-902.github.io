About 
======
I am an Electrical Engineer, Renewable Energy Professional, Data Analyst, Junior Data Scientist, Business Development Professional and Christian Leader with an interdisciplinary background that brings together engineering, technology, business and faith driven leadership.

Electrical Engineering 
------
I have a strong foundation in electrical power systems, electrical installations, energy management, maintenance, instrumentation and industrial systems. Through academic training and practical experience, I have developed an interest in solar energy and renewable energy technologies, with exposure to solar PV systems, grid-connected systems, energy audits, electrical inspection and maintenance and renewable energy projects. This experience has strengthened my passion for contributing to the transition toward reliable, affordable, and sustainable energy.

Business Development 
------
My journey has expanded into the financial and business sector, where I have worked as a Business Development Officer in banking/microfinance. This experience has exposed me to customer relationship management, business development, financial services, client onboarding, credit processes, market development, sales and stakeholder engagement. It has also strengthened my understanding of how technical knowledge, data, communication and business strategy can work together to create measurable organizational impact.

Leadership
------
 I have been involved in Christian leadership, ministry, media and production, worship, prayer, mentorship and community-oriented initiatives. These experiences have helped me develop qualities such as servant leadership, integrity, teamwork, discipline, communication, responsibility and a commitment to using my abilities to positively impact others.
