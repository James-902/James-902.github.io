I am an Electrical Engineer with a multidisciplinary background spanning electrical power systems, renewable energy, data analytics, artificial intelligence, business development, and Christian leadership. My professional journey has been driven by a desire to understand how technology, data, and people can work together to solve practical problems, improve decision-making, and create sustainable impact.

My foundation is in Electrical Engineering have enabled me develop a strong knowledge and practical experience in electrical power systems, substations, electrical installations, inspection and maintenance, energy management, instrumentation and power quality. Through academic training and practical exposure, I have worked with electrical systems ranging from low-voltage installations to high-voltage power infrastructure. My experience has included exposure to substation operations and maintenance, equipment testing and calibration, electrical inspections, energy audits, and electrical installation and maintenance activities. This is supported by being licenced by EPRA (C1 license)

My professional journey has also given me valuable exposure to the financial and business sector through my experience as a Business Development Officer in a bank/microfinance institution. This experience broadened my understanding beyond engineering and technology into customer relationships, financial services, business development, credit processes, client onboarding, loan appraisal, relationship management, sales, and market development.

Recognizing that modern engineering increasingly depends on data, I expanded my skill set into Data Analytics and Data Science. I trained in data analysis, data visualization, statistical analysis, machine learning, Python programming, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, pandas, and other data-related technologies.

Beyond my professional and academic interests, I am a devoted Christian and Christian leader.Through Christian leadership and ministry, I have had opportunities to lead teams, coordinate activities, facilitate meetings and discussions, participate in media production, mentor and encourage others, and serve within Christian communities.
